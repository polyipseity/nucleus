---
description: "Use when authoring markdown files. Bans hard-wrapping paragraphs for line-length limits, with the sole exception of validator/linter mandates."
name: "Markdown Line Wrapping"
applyTo: "**"
---

Do not insert hard line breaks to keep paragraphs under a certain column
width. Let the editor and renderer handle soft wrapping.

The only exception is when a validator or linter enforces a maximum line
length — in that case, follow the tool's requirement. No other justification
for hard-wrapping is valid.
